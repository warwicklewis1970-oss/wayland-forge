    Shape & position
    Cuts the shape with a straight through-line. The edge you cut off becomes a straight side of the piece, and the outside laser cut follows that closed outline.
    0 = through the centre (a half-circle on a circle). Positive moves the line toward the side you keep (you keep less); negative keeps more than half.
    Text on this layer
    Adds another line of text on this shape. No new outline or laser cut is made.
    Straight text: not attached to a shape. Drag its letters to move it.
    🖼 Image layers
    Image layers

    For laser photos: slide "Background to white" left until the grey background disappears (255 = off). Anything this light or lighter is not burnt.

    Letter edit
    Click a letter to select it. Shift/Ctrl/⌘-click, or switch Multi-select on, to add or remove letters anywhere. You can also drag a box on empty space. Esc clears the selection.

    Nudge
    0.6 mm is a modest start. This is artwork width, not a guaranteed burn width: laser spot and material change the result. Widened outlines use filled Line To Line bands, so they take longer than one outline pass. Extra width may close up small veins or berries; check preview and test scrap.
    Colours & background
    Click a swatch to colour the selected ornament or text layer.
